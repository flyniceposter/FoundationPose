How to explain the metrics

Recall means the percentage of cases passing a specified tolerance. ADD(-S) uses average model-point distance, accounting for symmetry for designated objects, with a cutoff of 10% of object diameter. MSSD uses symmetry-aware maximum surface distance with the same diameter-based cutoff. MSPD uses symmetry-aware maximum projected distance with a 5-pixel cutoff.

Say this carefully

“The 99.66% figure is our diagnostic pass rate with annotated object masks. It is not a claim of 99.66% accuracy in every real-world situation.”

Why the qualification matters

The masks are supplied by ground truth, rather than an automatic detector. Also, 5,186 baseline poses were recovered from rounded log text; 10,614 retain native precision. This is a recovered diagnostic baseline, not an official BOP benchmark submission.

The lower image-projection pass rate shows why one near-perfect metric does not describe every aspect of alignment quality.

FOUNDATIONPOSE / MEETING BRIEF · 3/5

Did imperfect masks cause problems?

For the perturbations tested, the main held-out pass rate stayed at 99.62%. Simple mask cleanup showed no improvement on that metric.

Controlled experiment

We used 520 frames across 13 objects: 20 development and 20 held-out frames per object. Each condition was repeated with three paired random seeds. Color, depth, model and pose settings were held fixed.

Thirteen mask conditions covered the original mask, shrinking or expanding boundaries by 2 or 5 pixels, and shifting by ±2 or ±5 pixels horizontally or vertically.

What cleanup was tested?

Development compared the original mask, opening with radius 2, and closing with radius 2. Opening can remove small protrusions; closing can fill small gaps. Development selected closing with radius 2 (“close2”) before held-out evaluation.

Intervals use 2,000 paired, object-stratified frame bootstrap samples. Cleanup changes summarize the held-out comparison across tested conditions. Equal pass rates do not mean individual poses or continuous errors are identical.

Interpretation

No demonstrated held-out benefit from this cleanup. The results support robustness to these particular small artificial mask errors. They do not establish robustness to large occlusions or realistic detector failures.

FOUNDATIONPOSE / MEETING BRIEF · 4/5

What is still running, and why?

Question: can we remove distracting input points?

The completed input audit found invalid depth inside object masks: 4.36% for the 520 BOP study frames, 18.60% for the mustard demo initialization mask, and 20.55% for the driller demo initialization mask. Existing preprocessing already rejects invalid depth.

About 1.71% of valid BOP masked depth was outside the largest connected mask region. These are possible filtering candidates; they are not proven bad points. Valid depth far from the median can also be real object geometry.

The active point-filter experiment

Four development methods are compared: unfiltered input, largest connected mask component, and two depth gates that retain depths near the median using half or one CAD-model diameter as the allowed distance.

The study uses the same 520 frames and three seeds, under six conditions: original mask, 5-pixel dilation, and 5-pixel shifts in four directions. Only initialization masks change; the full-scene depth input stays unchanged. Filtering uses depth and object size, not ground-truth poses.

The best development method is frozen before held-out evaluation. If unfiltered input wins, redundant held-out inference is skipped. Maximum work: 28,080 registrations.

Expected finish

The latest measured throughput suggests roughly 2½–3 hours from the approximately 11:10 p.m. Eastern snapshot: around 1:40–2:10 a.m. October 23, assuming uninterrupted execution. This is an estimate, not a completed result.

Why the NVIDIA demos are not a direct accuracy comparison

The demo registers once, then tracks subsequent frames. The BOP run registers every frame independently. The available demo sequences lack verified independent pose ground truth. Visual smoothness cannot be converted into a measured demo-versus-BOP accuracy advantage.

The audit examined input statistics on held-out frames, and the full baseline had already been inspected. These are exploratory study splits, not an untouched official benchmark test.
